Hi, I'm Shahid. I make music as C'sae: tropical and melodic house, mostly late at night, mostly in one small room with two speakers, a laptop and a couple of plants.

I started the channel in 2020 and kept posting the unfinished things: string experiments, progressive house loops, the bassline before the drop. In 2025 two of them became real singles, Journey into the unknown and Anymore.

Why house? Because therapy is expensive. A good drop does roughly the same job, and you can dance to it.

I'm open to collaborations, remixes, vocal features and live sets. If you sing and need a producer, or you run a night and need a sunset set, write to me.
